Packing & Moving Guides

Free, practical guides for planning a move — written by the people who built PackRight, the packing app for moving house. No fluff, no sponsored picks: just what works when you're standing in a room full of boxes.

Start with the checklist if your move is weeks away. Start with the kitchen or fragile guides if you're packing this weekend. And pack a First Night Essentials box either way — you'll thank yourself on moving night.

Moving Checklist: 8 Weeks to Moving Day

A week-by-week plan from eight weeks out to the first week in your new home — what to do, when to do it, and what not to leave until the last minute.

How to Declutter Before a Move

Keep, Donate, Sell, Discard — a room-by-room system for getting rid of things before you pack them, so you only move what you actually want.

How to Pack a Kitchen for Moving

The hardest room to pack, done right: what to pack first, how to wrap dishes and glasses, knives, small appliances, and the pantry.

How to Pack Fragile Items for Moving

The one rule that prevents most breakage, how to wrap and layer fragile things, and the shake test to run before you seal any box.

How to Label Boxes So Unpacking Is Easy

Number every box, write the room on the side, keep an inventory — a labelling system that turns unpacking from a treasure hunt into a checklist.

First Night Essentials Box — What to Pack

Bedding, toiletries, food, chargers, tools, and documents — everything you need for your first night and first morning in a new home, in one box.
