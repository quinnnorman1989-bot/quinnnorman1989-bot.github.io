PackRight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

PackRight ("the app") is published by the developer identified on the app's Google Play store listing ("we", "us"). Contact: quinn.norman1989@gmail.com.

The short version

PackRight stores your packing data on your device only. We run no servers and we never receive, transmit, or sell your packing data. The only network services involved are Google Sign-In (so the app knows who you are on your own device) and Stripe (only if you choose to subscribe to PackRight Pro).

What data the app handles

1. Sign-in information (Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address, and profile photo from Google so it can display your account inside the app. This information is stored only in your device's local storage. It is not sent to us or to any server we control. Sign-in is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

2. Packing data (rooms, items, boxes, photos, checklists, notes)

Everything you create in PackRight — including any photos you attach to items — is stored locally on your device (browser/app local storage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the in-app Export feature to download or share a backup file yourself. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this data is permanently deleted.

3. Payment information (PackRight Pro — optional)

Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe through a secure Stripe-hosted checkout page. We never see or store your card number or payment details. Stripe handles payment data under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. Pro status in this version of the app is recorded locally on your device.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no ad SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing
  • No background collection of location, contacts, or device identifiers
  • No account or data stored on any server operated by us

Permissions

The app may ask to access your camera or photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to an item. Attached photos are stored on your device only, as described above.

Children

PackRight is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
