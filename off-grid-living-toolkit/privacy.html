This privacy policy explains what the Off-Grid Living Toolkit collects, what it does not collect, and how advertising and cookies work on this site. It is written in plain language on purpose, because a policy nobody can understand is not much use to anybody.

What this site collects directly

This site collects nothing directly from you. There is no account to create, no sign-up form, no login, and no contact form on this site. You do not need to give your name, your email address, or any other personal information to use the calculators or read the guides. The calculators on the main page run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type into them — your stack size, your daily electricity use, your tank size, your water on hand — are used to work out the answer on your own device, and they are not sent to this site, to a server, or to anyone else.

Because there is no account and no form, this site has no list of users, no mailing list, and no way to contact you. If you want to get in touch about the site, there is no contact address published here, and that is deliberate: this is a free toolkit, not a service with support staff.

Advertising — Google AdSense

This site shows advertising through Google AdSense, with publisher ID ca-pub-5167099168803263. AdSense is the advertising service that lets this site stay free to use. Ads will only serve after this site is added to AdSense and approved — being wired for AdSense is not the same as being approved, and no claim is made here that approval has been given.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page, and you may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. This site itself does not set cookies of its own, because it has no login, no preferences to remember, and no shopping cart. The cookies on this site come from the advertising service described above, and from the hosting provider that serves the pages. Those cookies are used to serve and measure advertising, and to keep the site running.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies, delete cookies, or ask to be told when a cookie is set. Blocking cookies may change how advertising works on this site, but it will not stop the calculators or the guides from working, because those do not depend on cookies at all.

Other third parties and analytics

At the time this page was written, this site does not use a separate analytics service, a social media tracker, or a newsletter service. The only third-party code on the pages is the AdSense code in the page header and the advertising slots on the pages. If an analytics service or another third-party service is added later, this policy will be updated to say so, and the update will be dated.

Affiliate disclosure

There are no affiliate links on this site yet. That means none of the links on this site currently earn a commission, and none of them are paid placements. If affiliate links are added later, they will be for gear actually used off-grid, and they will be clearly labelled as affiliate links at the point where they appear. An affiliate link is a link where the site owner may earn a small commission if you buy something after clicking it, at no extra cost to you. This site will not use hidden affiliate links, and it will not present an affiliate link as an ordinary link.

The main toolkit page also says this in its "How this site makes money" section: advertising and, in time, affiliate links to gear actually used off-grid, clearly labelled when added, with no placeholder affiliate tag used until a real account is approved.

Your choices

You can use this site without giving it any personal information, because it does not ask for any. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings page or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page, as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. And you can simply stop using the site at any time — there is no account to close and nothing to delete, because nothing about you was collected here in the first place.
