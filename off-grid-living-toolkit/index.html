Winterizing checklist — the short version

  • Water: drain what can freeze, insulate exposed lines, and know how many days your totes actually last (calculator below).
  • Heat: seasoned wood only, chimney cleaned, a backup heat source that does not depend on the same fuel.
  • Power: generator serviced, fuel stabilised, batteries kept charged — a dead battery in -20 is a different problem than in summer.
  • Propane: any gas install or change must be done by a licensed gas fitter. Check local rules before you touch fittings.
  • Access: road, path, and a way to get help if a vehicle will not start. Winter makes small problems big.
